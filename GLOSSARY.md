# Forum operations

## Forum writing

**Original account** is the forum member identity associated with a submitted reply. A later sign-in can change which account the host is using.

**Confirmed reply** is a reply whose exact post has been independently read under the original account and whose author, stored content and relevant options match the submission. Two identical sends can match the same post.

**Uncertain send** is a submission whose effect on the forum remains unknown. The forum may have stored the reply even when the sender received no response.

**Moderation** means the forum reports that a submitted reply awaits review. Its publication is still pending.

## Notices

An **unread-notice poll** observes notices that the forum reports as unread and leaves their read state alone.

A **complete unread window** means the poll covered every unread notice in the requested time range for the same forum account and session. Older unread notices can still exist.

A **notice acknowledgement** is a separate, deliberate request to mark notices read on the forum.
