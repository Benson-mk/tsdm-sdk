**Deployed observation.** The [guest read receipt](guest-read.json) records the observation time, deployment, board, selected thread, HTTP responses and row counts. It covers a signed-out board-to-thread journey. Its `sha256` identifies the installed archive in the [candidate manifest](candidate.json). Member and privileged cases in this matrix use controlled inputs.

**Read the matrix.** `T/name` is `tests/install/name.mjs`. `G/name` is `docs/name.md`, with SDK and direct HTTP instructions unless a narrower section is named. Methods are called on the named package-root factory or the factory established earlier for that family. Quoted phrases identify case labels, fixtures or routes. `J` is document-free or structured JSON. `D` is JSON carrying usable HTML or an AJAX fragment. `H` is direct HTML. `X` is native XML/AJAX. `S17` is the structured small form. `W13` is the structured write result.

**Observational limits.** Controlled fixtures establish the stated request, response handling, or readback in that fixture. They leave real deployments, permissions, and persisted effects unobserved. For browser handoffs in the owner decision, such as registration, password recovery and trade history, the assertion covers the destination and any checked dispatch boundary. Browser completion remains unobserved. The avatar case observes the SDK supplying the host with the signed upload destination and form controls. It leaves upload and persistence unobserved.
